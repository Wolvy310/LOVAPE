# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Our Standards
Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
1. Demonstrating empathy and kindness.
2. Respecting differing opinions and experiences.
3. Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
4. Taking responsibility and learning from mistakes.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
1. Harassment, discrimination, or exclusionary language.
2. Trolling, insulting comments, personal attacks.
3. Publishing others' private information without consent.
4. Other conduct that is inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities
Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards
and will take fair corrective action in response to unacceptable behavior.

## Scope
This Code applies within all community spaces and when an individual is
representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement
Report incidents to the repository maintainer through private channels listed
in `SUPPORT.md`.
